# Quick – Instant Notes

**Official Support**

Quick is a lightweight, private notes app for macOS. Capture ideas instantly, find notes quickly, and keep your information on your Mac.

## Getting Started

- **Open Quick:** Press ⌥Space or use the menu bar icon.
- **Find a note:** Start typing in the search field.
- **Create a note:** Use Quick's new-note action.
- **Confidential notes:** Protect sensitive content and unlock it with Touch ID when available.
- **Backups:** Use Quick's import and export features to back up or restore your notes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does Quick require an account?**

No. Quick works locally without registration.

**Does Quick upload my notes?**

No. Quick stores your notes locally on your Mac.

**Can I recover deleted notes?**

Recently deleted notes can be recovered until they are permanently deleted.

**How do I protect confidential notes?**

Use Quick's confidential note feature to encrypt sensitive content and require authentication.
