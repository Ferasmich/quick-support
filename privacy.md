# Privacy Policy — Quick – Instant Notes

**Last updated: November 19, 2026**

Quick – Instant Notes is a macOS application developed by Feras Michael.

## 1. Data Collection

Quick does not collect personal information, analytics, or usage tracking data.

No account or registration is required.

## 2. Local Data Storage

Your notes are stored locally on your Mac. Quick does not upload your notes to external servers or provide cloud synchronization.

## 3. Confidential Notes

Quick provides encrypted confidential notes with local authentication, including Touch ID where supported.

Confidential note contents are protected using Apple's security technologies, including Keychain and Secure Enclave.

## 4. Backups

Quick supports importing and exporting notes. Backups are managed by the user and may be protected with encryption.

Quick does not upload backups to its own servers.

## 5. Third-Party Services

Quick does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking services.

## 6. Data Deletion

You can delete notes within Quick, including permanently deleting notes from Recently Deleted.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when Quick's functionality or privacy practices change. Updates will be published on this page.

## 8. Contact

For privacy questions or support, visit the [Quick Support repository](https://github.com/Ferasmich/quick-support/issues).
